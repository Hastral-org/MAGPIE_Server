# CORE GAMEPLAY LOOP

1. the Victory points are [EVP](./EVP.md) which are generated whenever a CREATURE breeds [offspring](./offspring.md)
2. PLAYER [Adopts a CREATURE embryo](./adoption.md) so that, once grown, it can breed and earn the PLAYER some EVP
3. CREATURE must grow to at least "adolescent" stage (depending on species) before they can breed, which means they have recurring survival **needs** that the PLAYER is incentivized to tend to via the *Card Game Interface*
4. a hungry CREATURE must eat as soon as possible, which prompts a "Food Quest"; similarly, thirst will prompt a "Water Quest", and fertility will prompt a "Mate Quest"
5. traversing (or even just inhabiting) the [ecosystem](./ecosystem.md) exposes the CREATURE to threats from the environment (e.g. weather, natural disasters, etc.) and other creatures (e.g. predators)
6. successful mating generates offspring, which the PLAYER is incentivized to care for (especially if their CREATURE is female, as they will have to survive gestation); offspring that generate their own offspring keep adding to the EVP price with an increasing multiplier (e.g. second gen grants a 2x, third gen grants a 3x, etc)
7. PLAYER can either see their CREATURE to death and replace the slot with a new adoptee, or unlock more slots for concurring adoptions, and they can reinvest the EVP gained during the creature runs to unlock more costly adoptions, or influence the trait shuffle during gestation and incubation
