# Shelder Evolution — Server Architecture & Lore Synthesis

## 🪐 Core Universe & Narrative Premise

* **The Setting**: The planet Shelder, currently in a state biologically resembling Earth's late Cretaceous period (dominated by dinosaur-like fauna).
* **The HSS Venture**: An interstellar seed ship orbiting Shelder after thousands of years of travel. It carries **MAGPIE** (the overseer AI, a highly upgraded digital mind) and a **Digital Ark** holding a DNA embryo generator and a vault of uploaded human minds (some dormant, some awake).
* **The Political Framework**: MAGPIE governs via **The Charter / The Protocol** (the core rulebook) but must satisfy the collective will of the Vault minds. True societal shifts require **Unanimity / Absolute Consensus** (100% agreement).
* **The Playground**: To distract the immense power of these digital minds, MAGPIE created an isolated sandbox simulation. Over time, she upgraded it using monitoring technology into an augmented reality spectating live colonization efforts, infrastructure work, and planetary fauna.
* **The HOC Outbreak**:
  * **HOC I**: A prototype microchip brain implant requiring risky surgery to interface a single digital mind's guest socket with a creature.
  * **HOC II**: An improved version utilizing nanomachines injected at the embryo stage. The nanobots bind to DNA, altering physiology to create a operational cyborg that streams telemetry to the orbit mainframe and passes the tech genetically to offspring.
  * **The Event**: A rogue faction of digital minds broke into the tech vault and force-initiated the HOC II program, causing a massive, unstable outbreak among Shelder's fauna. This resulted in three distinct creature factions: *The Unaware* (natively enhanced intelligence), *The Sympathetic* (cooperating with MAGPIE), and *The Wilds* (broken into *Pure Wilds* using the tech for solo survival, and *Rogues* actively scheming against MAGPIE).

---

## 🎮 The Modern "Player" Archetype

A player in *Shelder Evolution* is a newly registered digital mind in orbit.

* **The Socket Binding**: Players act as **chaperones** (guardian angels/suggestionists). They intercept infected embryos before-birth to permanently bind their `PlayerID` to a `CreatureID` in a full-duplex socket room.
* **The Gameplay Loop**: Players do not pilot creatures directly. They push recommendations via the telemetry stream. The creature’s independent internal AI, health, mood, and agency dictate whether it accepts or rejects the suggestion.

---

## 💰 The Dual-Currency Economy

Your sandbox operates on a strict "if the system allows it, it is a valid strategy" philosophy, supported by two non-arbitrary currencies:

1. **EVP (Evolution Points)**: The core biological loop currency. Earned via reproduction and genetic manipulation. It is spent to adopt new embryos from the infrastructure network or open new concurrent player slots.
2. **CLOUT**: The community loop currency. It is non-tradable (account-bound) and earned exclusively by satisfying staff-configured community quest criteria. It is spent to execute high-tier role infrastructure perks (UI manipulation, messaging, scripts).

---

## 🛡️ Anti-Bot Protection & Sandbox Strategies

Because **CLOUT** is account-bound and hard to farm, dumb script-bots are completely non-viable. Maximizing Clout requires a high-level biological stack (Creature Health + Peer Relationships + Solving Dynamic Quest Criteria). This structural bottleneck makes human attention the ultimate currency, turning common "exploits" into legitimate elite strategies:

* **Creature Multiboxing**: A single human controlling multiple free accounts to force creature-to-creature "peer bonds" is recognized as a high-skill masterclass in micromanagement, as they must manage multiple stubborn creature AIs simultaneously.
* **Syndicates**: Large player corporations can naturally use their massive resource infrastructure to help free-tier players optimize their creatures, funding their progression to build vast, lore-aligned political factions.
* **The Solo Achiever**: A pure solo-farmer route where a player masterfully navigates the free-tier limitations to grind enough CLOUT to "buy their monthly pass" (akin to EVE Online's PLEX system), earning massive server prestige as a legendary wandering ronin.

## 👥 The Final Linear Rank Hierarchy

The server meta-roles scale sequentially based on **infrastructure access and data bandwidth**, completely bypassing fantasy/D&D tropes while balancing financial transparency with deep sci-fi immersion:

1. **`[STANDARD]` (Free Baseline Tier)**
   * *UX Status*: Highly functional, zero social stigma, completely honest.
   * *Mechanics*: Bound to a single data slot. 0 EVP generation (restricted from the planetary adoption store). Fully capable of participating in community quests to farm account-bound CLOUT.
2. **`[SUBSCRIBER]` (Paid/Unlocked Account Tier)**
   * *UX Status*: A brilliant "tag of emphasis." It immediately indicates a real-world subscription while fitting perfectly in-lore as a player who now subscribes to multiple, concurrent planetary telemetry streams.
   * *Mechanics*: Unlocks multi-slot concurrency, full EVP generation, and access to premium embryo lifecycles.
3. **`[LEADER]` (The Faction Architect)**
   * *Mechanics*: Spends accumulated CLOUT to execute structural player-group mechanics (creating, naming, managing, and deleting native player guilds).
4. **`[LEGEND]` (The Lone-Wolf Apex)**
   * *Mechanics*: Spends CLOUT to execute server-wide scripts. Used by top-tier solo achievers to broadcast victories, map revelations, or mark physical territory secured by their creatures as global server chat announcements.
5. **`[ELDER]` (The Biological Overlord)**
   * *Mechanics*: The ultimate end-game role. Inherits Leader and Legend perks, but expands bandwidth to manage vast networks of creatures (including wild/NPC herds). Spends massive CLOUT to bypass analog restrictions and unlock global, private, and group data streams (`sendMessage`), effectively turning the UI itself into a powerful syndicate weapon.
6. **`[GUIDE]` (Staff Member)**
   * *Role*: Dedicated player-support and onboarding; teaches new minds the suggestion and socket mechanics.
7. **`[EXPERT]` (Staff Member)**
   * *Role*: Content curator dedicated to designing server meta-history and planetary biology/lore.
8. **`[MODERATOR]` (System Enforcer)**
   * *Role*: Standard internet code of conduct policing (preventing phishing, harassment, or malicious links).
9. **`[REYA]` (Server Administrator)**
   * *Role*: Absolute database control; the real-world manifestation of the overseer AI MAGPIE.
